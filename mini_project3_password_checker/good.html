Good Passwords

A good password is long, random, and unique — not clever. It should be at least 12–16 characters, mixing upper and lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols, but length matters more than complexity for resisting brute-force attacks. It shouldn't contain real words, names, birthdays, or anything guessable from your social media, since attackers often try personal details first. Critically, a good password is never reused across multiple accounts, because if one site gets breached, reused passwords let attackers break into everything else you use that password for. In practice, the best approach is letting a password manager generate and store long random strings for you, since no one can reliably memorize dozens of truly random passwords — the one password worth memorizing well is the master password protecting that vault.
