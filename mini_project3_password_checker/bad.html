Bad Passwords

A bad password is short, predictable, and personal. Think "password123", "qwerty", a pet's name, a birthday, or a favourite sports team. Anything a stranger could guess after looking at your social media could be a bad password. Bad passwords can also be used on multiple websites, which means if one account gets hacked, other accounts could also be at risk. Other bad passwords include simple changes like "P@ssw0rd", passwords that are very short, and passwords using repeated or sequential characters. If a password is very easy to remember, it could also be easy for someone else to guess.
