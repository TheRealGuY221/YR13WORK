Rarest Skins

The rarest Fortnite skins are some of the most valuable and sought-after cosmetics in the game, especially among longtime players and collectors. Skins such as Black Widow, Travis Scott, Galaxy, Honor Guard, Rue, and the original Renegade Raider have gained legendary status because they were either available for a very limited time, tied to special promotions, or haven't returned for years. Renegade Raider and Aerial Assault Trooper were once considered the ultimate OG skins, although they returned during Fortnite OG in 2024, making them less exclusive than they used to be. Even so, rare skins remain popular because they represent different eras of Fortnite and can show how long someone has been playing the game
