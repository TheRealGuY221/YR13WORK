Healing items

In Fortnite, healing items are very important because they help you recover health and shield after taking damage. Some of the best healing items are Chug Splashes, because they heal quickly and can also heal teammates, and Slurp-type items, because they can restore both health and shield. Med Kits are also good when your health is very low, while Big Shield Potions are useful for getting a large amount of shield back. Mini Shield Potions are great for quickly getting some shield back, especially during a fight. Med-Mist can be useful because you can spray yourself or teammates, but it can take time to use. Bandages are one of the weaker healing items because they only restore a small amount of health. Overall, the best heals are usually the ones that work quickly and restore lots of health or shield, while slow or weak healing items are generally worse, especially when you are being attacked.


Weapons

Some of the best weapons in Fortnite are assault rifles, shotguns, and SMGs because they are useful in different types of fights. Shotguns are especially powerful at close range and can deal a lot of damage quickly, making them great for close-quarters combat. Assault rifles are good for medium- and sometimes long-range fights because they are accurate and have a good balance of damage and fire rate. SMGs are useful at close range because they fire quickly and can break through an opponent’s health and shields fast. Sniper rifles can also be very strong when used by players who are good at aiming, as they can deal huge damage from far away. Overall, the best weapon depends on your playstyle, but carrying a strong shotgun along with a reliable assault rifle or SMG is usually a great combination.
